Project overview

The Barangay Binlod Issuance-Monitoring System brings barangay staff operations and resident services into one system. Its three separate portals—Captain, Secretary, and Resident—provide different tools and permissions for each user group. Residents can request and track certificates themselves, while authorized staff manage records and process requests.

Main functionalities
  • Resident services: Update profile information, request certificates, view fees, choose a payment method, upload payment proof when needed, and track request status and history.
  • Certificate processing: Staff manage certificate types, fees, and issuance, with request progress visible to residents.
  • Barangay records: Manage resident records, officials, blotter cases, and archives.
  • Administration and reporting: Manage staff accounts, review audit activity, generate reports, and handle resident profile update requests.
  • Account protection: Role-based access, login-attempt lockouts, and account recovery features help protect system access.
Essentials for users
  • Residents: Sign in with a registered account, keep profile details current, submit a certificate request with the required details, and check its status in the portal. Residents who are not registered should contact the Barangay Hall.
  • Secretary: Sign in to manage assigned resident and service workflows, review requests, and assist with day-to-day barangay operations.
  • Captain: Oversee system activity, manage staff accounts and access, review records, and use reporting and audit features.
Benefits
  • Reduces reliance on paper-based request tracking.
  • Gives residents a convenient way to submit and follow up on certificate requests.
  • Helps staff organize resident information and service workflows.
  • Makes request status and administrative actions easier to monitor.
  • Limits access to sensitive functions based on user roles.
